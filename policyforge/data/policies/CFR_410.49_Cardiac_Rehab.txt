Cardiac Rehabilitation Programs
42 CFR § 410.49

Effective Date: February 18, 2014 (chronic heart failure added)

A. General

Medicare Part B covers cardiac rehabilitation (CR) and intensive cardiac rehabilitation (ICR) program services for beneficiaries who meet eligibility criteria and are referred by their treating physician.

B. Covered Conditions

Medicare Part B covers cardiac rehabilitation and intensive cardiac rehabilitation for beneficiaries who have experienced one or more of the following:

1. An acute myocardial infarction within the preceding 12 months
2. A coronary artery bypass surgery
3. Current stable angina pectoris
4. Heart valve repair or replacement
5. Percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty (PTCA) or coronary stenting
6. A heart or heart-lung transplant
7. Stable, chronic heart failure defined as patients with:
   - Left ventricular ejection fraction of 35 percent or less AND
   - New York Heart Association (NYHA) class II to IV symptoms AND
   - Despite being on optimal heart failure therapy for at least 6 weeks
   (Effective on or after February 18, 2014 for CR; February 9, 2018 for ICR)
8. Other cardiac conditions as specified through a national coverage determination (NCD)

C. Required Program Components

Cardiac rehabilitation and intensive cardiac rehabilitation must include ALL of the following:

1. Physician-prescribed exercise each day cardiac rehabilitation items and services are furnished
2. Cardiac risk factor modification, including education, counseling, and behavioral intervention
3. Psychosocial assessment
4. Outcomes assessment

D. Coverage Settings

Medicare Part B pays for cardiac rehabilitation and intensive cardiac rehabilitation in the following settings:

1. Physician's office
2. Hospital outpatient department
3. Critical access hospital (outpatient)
4. Other settings as determined by the Secretary

E. Frequency Limitations

1. Cardiac Rehabilitation (Standard):
   - Maximum of 2 one-hour sessions per day
   - Up to 36 sessions over up to 36 weeks
   - Option for additional 36 sessions over extended period if approved by Medicare Administrative Contractor

2. Intensive Cardiac Rehabilitation:
   - Up to 72 one-hour sessions
   - Maximum of 6 sessions per day
   - Over a period of up to 18 weeks

F. Physician Requirements

The physician responsible for the cardiac rehabilitation program must:

1. Be trained in the management of individuals with cardiac pathophysiology
2. Have clinical expertise with emergency management of acutely ill cardiac patients
3. Be licensed to practice medicine in the State in which the cardiac rehabilitation program is offered

G. Coverage Codes

Covered HCPCS/CPT codes include:

- 93797: Physician or other qualified health care professional services for cardiac rehabilitation
- 93798: Physician or other qualified health care professional services for cardiac rehabilitation; with continuous ECG monitoring

H. Non-Covered Services

- Sessions exceeding the frequency limitations
- Services provided in non-approved settings
- Programs that do not include all required components
- Programs not supervised by qualified physicians
